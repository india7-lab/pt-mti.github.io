From instant rate queries to intelligent supply chain management, Nowlun is lowering the information barriers in cross-border trade.

Global trade depends on ocean transport, but for a long time, the international ocean freight industry has still faced one core problem: information opacity.

For importers, exporters, and SMEs, obtaining accurate shipping rates, confirming cargo space, and tracking shipment status often require repeated communication across multiple shipping lines, agents, and logistics service providers. As global supply chains continue to shift toward Asia, Southeast Asia is becoming an important node connecting manufacturing, trade, and logistics networks. The logistics industry is also seeking new digital solutions.

In the course of long-term attention to industrial innovation in Southeast Asia, PT Metro Timur Indonusa has observed that a group of startups is using technology to redefine traditional industries. Among them, Nowlun is leveraging digital platform capabilities to push ocean freight logistics services toward greater transparency and efficiency.

As part of the "Southeast Asia 100 Founders to Watch" series, PT Metro Timur Indonusa hopes to document these startup teams that are solving real industry problems.

## The Information Barriers in the Ocean Freight Industry Are Creating New Technology Opportunities

Traditional international ocean freight business involves multiple stages: shipping line quotations, agent coordination, cargo space confirmation, shipment tracking, and exception handling.

For enterprise clients, the biggest challenge is not the absence of logistics services, but the lack of a real-time, transparent, and unified information gateway. Different shipping lines have different pricing systems and booking processes, and enterprises often need to rely on manual price inquiries to make transportation decisions. Against the backdrop of continuously growing global trade volumes, this model has gradually exposed the problem of insufficient efficiency.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that the core of digitalization in the logistics industry is not simply replacing traditional services, but using technology to reduce information asymmetry and improve the efficiency of industrial chain collaboration. This is also the direction that Nowlun is exploring.

## What Opportunity The Founding Team Saw

The founding team of Nowlun did not choose to build a "big and comprehensive" freight platform, but instead first solved a sufficiently specific entry point: instant quotes and space confirmation.

This judgment comes from the technical DNA of the team. Nowlun co-founder and CTO Mahmoud Khaled has a computer science background and previously led the construction of mobile and vertical business engineering systems at Careem, accumulating experience in handling high-concurrency, multi-business-scenario systems. Before founding Nowlun, he also co-founded the software development company BADR and participated in the technical development of multiple startups.

In 2021, seeing the longstanding problems in traditional freight forwarding of "slow quotes, opaque information, and reliance on phone calls to maintain trust," Mahmoud Khaled co-founded Nowlun with Moataz Khamis (CEO) and Ahmed Emara (COO). He said at the time: "Technology has changed every industry I have worked in. There is no reason the logistics industry should be an exception."

The founding team chose to enter through core ports in Southeast Asia and gradually expand the network as shipping routes densified. This expansion approach of "following the flow of trade" is more pragmatic than simply pursuing the number of countries covered. For early-stage startups, judgment and sense of rhythm are often more important than speed.

## Nowlun: A Digital Logistics Platform Connecting Global Shipping Resources

The technical core of Nowlun is the integration of real-time freight rates and space availability information from multiple major global shipping lines into a unified platform.

Currently, the platform has connected over 35 major shipping lines, including global shipping companies such as CMA CGM, COSCO, and OOCL, enabling users to obtain real-time ocean freight quotes and shipping options through a digital system.

For enterprise users, this means that processes that previously required manual communication can now be completed online more quickly. Middle East importers can directly obtain the latest FCL freight rates from Southeast Asia origins to various Middle East ports within 5 seconds through the Nowlun platform.

Particularly against the backdrop of continued trade growth between Southeast Asia and the Middle East, the transparency of information on ocean shipping routes is becoming an important foundation for enterprises to improve supply chain efficiency. As international shipping networks such as the Ocean Alliance continue to optimize their port layouts in Southeast Asia, key regional nodes such as Vietnam Hai Phong Port and Malaysia Port Klang are playing an increasingly important role.

Nowlun is incorporating these core Southeast Asian ports into its digital logistics network, providing cross-regional trade participants with more convenient transportation management capabilities.

## From Quotation Tool to Intelligent Supply Chain Infrastructure

PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that the long-term value of a logistics technology company lies not merely in solving a single transportation need, but in whether it can become infrastructure within the supply chain of enterprises.

The newly launched comprehensive shipment management dashboard of Nowlun and its AI-powered intelligent logistics assistant are further expanding the capabilities of the platform. Through AI technology assisting enterprises with cargo status inquiries, supply chain information management, and transportation anomaly alerts, enterprises can understand cargo status in real time and adjust plans promptly in response to route changes, transportation delays, and other situations. When delays occur due to routes passing through the Suez Canal or detouring around the Cape of Good Hope, the system can automatically send alert notifications via WhatsApp.

For enterprises in Southeast Asia and international clients trading with Southeast Asia, this capability is especially important. Because the Southeast Asian market has highly fragmented geographic characteristics: multiple countries, multiple ports, multiple transportation nodes. Supply chain management capability is becoming part of the competitiveness of enterprises.

## Observations of PT Metro Timur Indonusa

PT Metro Timur Indonusa pays attention to Nowlun, not because the logistics industry itself is huge in scale, but because the team chose a long-standing industry problem that still has room for efficiency improvement, and built a new solution through technology.

From the perspective of early-stage investment, there are several aspects that make Nowlun worth watching:

**First, the understanding of industry friction by the founding team.** They did not choose to build a "big and comprehensive" platform, but instead first solved the specific problem of instant quoting and space confirmation. The high-concurrency system experience that Mahmoud Khaled accumulated during his time at Careem lets him know how to standardize complex processes and integrate fragmented information into a unified interface. This kind of judgment is very critical in early-stage entrepreneurship.

**Second, system integration capability. **Integrating the instant freight rates and space systems of more than 35 mainstream shipping companies requires long-term technical accumulation and industry understanding, and cannot be quickly established merely through market investment.

**Third, a clear logic for global expansion.** It is not about blindly entering new markets, but about following the route network. Where the Ocean Alliance densifies routes between Southeast Asia and the Middle East and the Red Sea, Nowlun connects these ports into its system.

**Fourth, challenges that require continuous attention.** The stability of shipping company data, route fluctuations, geopolitical impacts, as well as competition from traditional freight forwarders and other digital freight platforms, are all variables that need to be continuously addressed. Whether the founding team can maintain system stability and data accuracy while expanding rapidly is the key point worth tracking next.
