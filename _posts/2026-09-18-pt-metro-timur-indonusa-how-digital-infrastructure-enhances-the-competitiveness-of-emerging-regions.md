Digital infrastructure is a key driver for Indonesia to enhance its digital competitiveness. However, PT Metro Timur Indonusa has identified a more noteworthy conclusion in its recent research: substantive improvements in competitiveness occur only when infrastructure development advances in parallel with digital governance and technology adoption across both the public and private sectors.

This assessment is consistent with the core findings of the 2026 Southeast Asia Digital Economy Investment Trends Report previously released by PT Metro Timur Indonusa. The report focuses on analyzing how artificial intelligence, fintech, and digital infrastructure drive regional industrial upgrading, and points out that Indonesia occupies an important position in the future technology investment ecosystem.

In other words, simply building base stations is not enough; people must use them, rules must govern them, and businesses must be built on them.

For investment institutions focused on entrepreneurial opportunities in emerging regions, this is an important basis for judgment. PT Metro Timur Indonusa has long paid attention to the digital entrepreneurship ecosystem in Southeast Asian second- and third-tier markets, and this study provides a set of real data from provinces across Indonesia, allowing us to see the interconnected relationship among infrastructure, governance, and commercial activity.

## Southwest Papua: Once the Network Is Connected, Transactions Take Off

The most notable change in the study comes from Southwest Papua. This youngest province in Indonesia jumped 15 places in the 2026 digital competitiveness ranking, rising from 33rd nationwide to 18th, with its score increasing from 34.9 to 43.8.

The core driver behind this change was the expansion of digital infrastructure—broader 4G network coverage and the introduction of 5G networks in the Sorong area. Once connectivity improved, the growth of economic activity followed almost automatically: QRIS transaction volume increased by 280.6% year-on-year, while the number of QRIS merchants grew by 24.5%.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that the case of Southwest Papua illustrates a simple yet easily overlooked point: in many emerging regions, entrepreneurs do not lack business acumen; they lack the basic conditions to digitize their businesses. Once networks are connected and payment tools become available, local commercial activity can grow at a pace beyond expectations. For early-stage investors, this means that in regions where infrastructure has just improved, there is a group of entrepreneurs “waiting to be connected.”

## South Sulawesi: Competitiveness Becomes Stable Only When Governance Keeps Pace

The case of South Sulawesi demonstrates another dimension of change.

The province returned to the national top ten, rising one place to 10th, with a score of 48.9. This progress mainly came from the development of digital government—the “regional government regulation and capacity” indicator increased by more than 20 percentage points, benefiting from improvements in e-government systems, faster digital transactions by local governments, and stronger cybersecurity measures.

At the same time, the economy of the South Sulawesi grew by 6.9% in the first quarter of 2026, with the contribution of the information and communications sector continuing to expand.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that the experience of South Sulawesi shows that after infrastructure is built, governance capacity determines the sustainability of growth. If local governments can digitize public services and move transaction processes online, the friction costs of doing business will decline, allowing the vitality of the digital economy to be truly released. For investment institutions, the level of governance in a region is becoming an important reference for assessing whether that region is worth entering.

## East Kalimantan: Internet Access Reaches Villages, and MSMEs Benefit

East Kalimantan rose two places in the rankings, placing sixth nationwide with a score of 52.4, making it the only province outside Java to enter the top six.

The “Free Village Internet” project initiated by the East Kalimantan provincial government had covered 802 of 841 villages by the end of 2025, effectively expanding the access for villagers to digital public services, education, and marketing opportunities for micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises. The project promoted the development of MSMEs and helped drive annual bank credit growth to 17.4%.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa pays particular attention to this case because it directly connects to the group we care about most—MSMEs. Once internet access reaches villages, the first beneficiaries are often small merchants that could previously only conduct local business. Through e-commerce platforms, they can sell products to more distant markets; through digital payment tools, they can establish credit records and subsequently obtain bank loans. This process is not a theoretical deduction, but something that is truly happening in East Kalimantan.

## Central Java and East Java: Infrastructure Is the Foundation, Economic Activity Is the Outcome

On the island of Java, East Java ranked third nationwide with a score of 58.0, benefiting from improved digital infrastructure quality, the expansion of 4G base stations, and the integration of digital public services.

Central Java climbed to eighth place with a score of 51.7, expanding free internet access to 327 villages, including remote areas and areas of extreme poverty. The province achieved economic growth of 5.4%, while realized investment increased by 28.9%, and all 35 regencies and cities recorded local government transaction electrification index scores above 90%.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that the data of Central Java reflects a clear trend: when infrastructure, governance, and economic participation form a positive cycle, the improvement of regional competitiveness accelerates significantly. Expanded internet coverage, digitized local government transactions, and follow-up business investment—none of these three links can be missing.

## AI and Fintech: The Next Step for the Digital Economy in Emerging Regions

On the basis of gradually improving infrastructure and governance, PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that AI and fintech are becoming important forces driving the digital economy in emerging regions into its next stage.

Over the past decade, the growth of Southeast Asian digital economy was mainly driven by e-commerce, mobile payments, and the consumer internet. As artificial intelligence technology matures rapidly, AI is shifting from an auxiliary tool into an important foundational capability for improving enterprise efficiency, upgrading financial services, and advancing industrial digitalization.

For emerging regions in Indonesia, the combination of AI and fintech carries particular significance. Because there are still large populations with insufficient access to financial services, as well as substantial digitalization needs among small and medium-sized enterprises in these regions, AI technology can help financial institutions reduce service costs, improve risk identification capabilities, and expand the coverage of financial services.

## PT Metro Timur Indonusa has observed in its research that future technology investment opportunities in Indonesia will mainly be concentrated in three areas:

First, AI-driven intelligent risk control and digital credit. In the traditional financial system, micro and small enterprises as well as young consumers often find it difficult to access financial services because some users lack complete credit records. AI technology can help financial institutions build more accurate risk assessment systems by analyzing data across more dimensions.

Second, embedded finance and enterprise digitalization services. As the digital economy develops, financial services are gradually being integrated into more commercial scenarios. For the large number of small and medium-sized enterprises in Indonesia, there remains substantial demand for digital procurement, supply chain management, and enterprise financial services.

Third, digital banking and intelligent financial service upgrades. Financial institutions that can combine AI technology to improve customer acquisition efficiency, customer service, and asset management capabilities will have stronger long-term competitiveness.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that AI enterprises with genuine long-term value must possess both technological capabilities and commercial implementation capabilities. Having technological advantages alone is not enough to form a competitive barrier; enterprises must also understand user needs, adapt to market conditions, and transform technology into real business value.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa will continue to monitor changes in digital infrastructure and governance capabilities across Indonesian regions, seeking entrepreneurs who are solving real problems in areas where “connections have just been established.”
