The cost of employee turnover is typically equivalent to 1.5 to 2 times the employee annual salary. For early-stage investment institutions, team stability directly affects the execution capabilities and long-term value creation of a company.

As an early-stage investment platform based in Southeast Asia, PT Metro Timur Indonusa has repeatedly observed one issue through its post-investment services: many startups devote substantial effort to fundraising, product development, and market expansion, yet underestimate the erosion of long-term corporate value caused by talent loss.

For early-stage investment institutions, team stability affects not only day-to-day operations but also the execution capabilities, speed of innovation, and capacity for long-term value creation of a company. As competition for talent intensifies, companies must not only attract outstanding candidates but also make every effort to retain those who have already expressed an intention to join.

Based on its early-stage investment practices and observations from post-investment management, PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that organizational capability in talent management is an important factor influencing the long-term growth of startups. The following section summarizes, from an investment perspective, the key factors that companies should consider when developing their talent strategies.

## I. A Competitive Compensation System: A Foundational Capability for Startups

Total compensation includes not only base salaries and bonuses but also equity incentives, healthcare benefits, retirement plans, and additional paid leave. A well-designed compensation package can significantly reduce employee turnover.

For prospective employees, a transparent compensation structure and a clear performance-based bonus mechanism can quickly establish trust. Companies should regularly benchmark their compensation against industry standards to avoid losing outstanding candidates over relatively minor differences. Employees place greater value on fairness—equal pay for equal work often fosters stronger long-term loyalty than high salaries alone.

When evaluating portfolio companies, PT Metro Timur Indonusa examines whether their compensation systems are appropriately structured, as this directly affects team stability and recruitment efficiency. Compensation is not merely a cost item; it is also fundamental infrastructure for organizational capability.

## II. Organizational Growth Paths Determine the Long-Term Value of Talent

Many people leave their jobs not because they are underpaid, but because they cannot see a future.

Companies can address this issue through regular career discussions, personalized development plans, and policies that prioritize internal promotion. Companies that provide clear opportunities for growth have significantly lower first-year turnover among new employees. Businesses need to shift from merely “employing people” to “developing people” by investing in training, mentorship programs, and cross-departmental rotations, allowing prospective employees to feel genuinely valued and supported.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that whether a company enables employees to envision where they will be in three years is an important indicator of organizational health. In its post-investment support, the platform frequently reminds founders that career development paths are not employee benefits; they are a core component of competitiveness in talent.

## III. Organizational Flexibility Is Becoming a Competitive Advantage for Startups

Remote work, flexible working hours, and compressed workweeks have become widely accepted. Companies that support flexible arrangements experience notable improvements in employee satisfaction and retention. For prospective employees, flexibility is often a decisive factor in whether they accept an offer.

Companies need to shift their focus from hours worked to results achieved and trust employees to manage themselves. Although this requires changes in management practices, the resulting improvements in productivity and attractiveness to talent far outweigh the associated costs.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa has observed that organizational flexibility is particularly important for Southeast Asian startups, as many teams are distributed across different cities or even different countries. Companies capable of managing distributed teams effectively have a clear advantage in the competition for talent.

## IV. Corporate Culture Determines Long-Term Team Stability

Culture is not defined by occasional team-building activities, but by the respect, transparency, and psychological safety employees experience every day. Employees want to be heard, recognized, and able to be their authentic selves.

Companies can strengthen their culture through open communication channels, diverse hiring practices, and anti-bias training. A strong cultural fit can significantly reduce voluntary turnover. Founders and management must lead by example and help new employees integrate quickly rather than allowing them to become isolated.

In its post-investment services, PT Metro Timur Indonusa frequently reminds founders that culture is not a slogan written on a wall; it is what employees experience every day. When evaluating portfolio companies, the platform assesses whether their culture genuinely supports long-term team stability.

## V. Talent Recognition Mechanisms Affect Organizational Efficiency

Regular feedback, public praise, and peer-recognition systems are powerful sources of motivation. Employees who receive frequent recognition are significantly less likely to leave.

Companies should establish multilevel recognition mechanisms, ranging from everyday verbal praise to formal awards, rather than reserving feedback solely for year-end evaluations. Prospective employees are particularly sensitive during their first three months—positive recognition at an early stage often determines whether they remain with the company over the long term.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that recognition does not need to cost money, but it does require thought and attention. Many founders underestimate this point, even though it is precisely one of the organizational capability-building areas most easily overlooked in post-investment support.

## VI. A Sense of Mission Helps Companies Attract Long-Term Partners

Prospective employees increasingly want their efforts to make a genuine impact on both the company and society. Companies can cultivate this through mission-driven projects, social responsibility initiatives, and employee participation in decision-making.

Companies with a clearly defined mission often achieve employee loyalty far above the industry average. Businesses need to shift from “assigning tasks” to “communicating purpose,” helping employees connect their personal values with the company objectives.

When screening investment opportunities, PT Metro Timur Indonusa considers whether founders can clearly articulate the company mission and whether the team genuinely identifies with that mission. A sense of mission is not a soft benefit; it is a key capability for attracting long-term partners.

## VII. Learning Capacity Determines the Ability of an Organization to Adapt to Future Change

Providing course subsidies, external training, and internal knowledge-sharing opportunities enables employees to see that the company is sincerely investing in their future. Companies offering abundant learning opportunities achieve significantly higher retention rates.

Learning should become part of everyday work rather than an occasional benefit, enabling prospective employees to experience support for their development from their first day.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that, in the rapidly changing markets across Southeast Asia, learning capacity is itself a form of competitiveness. Companies willing to invest in employee learning are more likely to prevail in long-term competition. This is also an aspect of organizational capability that the platform continues to prioritize in its post-investment support.

## Observations of PT Metro Timur Indonusa

For PT Metro Timur Indonusa, talent development is not an isolated component of corporate management, but an important factor in assessing the capacity of a startup for long-term growth.

The key to retaining prospective employees lies in a change in corporate mindset: talent must be treated as the company most valuable asset rather than as a cost. Management needs to actively listen to feedback and optimize policies, while human resources departments should evolve from administrative functions into strategic partners. The results will become fully apparent only when talent retention becomes a company-wide commitment rather than solely the responsibility of HR.

Through its investment activities, PT Metro Timur Indonusa has observed that companies capable of retaining talent demonstrate stronger risk resilience and greater potential for sustained growth. These companies understand that outstanding employees are not produced through “management” alone, but through attraction, motivation, and investment. Businesses capable of building strong organizational capabilities are more likely to continue creating value in highly competitive emerging markets.
