As an early-stage investment platform based in Southeast Asia, PT Metro Timur Indonusa has long focused on energy transition, green industries, and changes in regional industrial structures, while observing development opportunities in emerging markets through industry research and industrial exchanges.

On August 14, 2026, Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto publicly stated that Indonesian carbon exchange has adopted global carbon standards and expressed hope that the carbon trading system could create greater economic value for the country and society. This statement once again drew market attention to the development of Indonesian carbon market, and the share prices of several companies owning renewable energy generation assets subsequently fluctuated.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa hopes to discuss three core questions from an investment perspective: What does carbon credit monetization mean for new energy companies? Which asset types are more likely to generate carbon credit revenue? How long will it take for such revenue to be realized?

## How The Carbon Market Changes The Value Logic Of New Energy Companies  

In the past, the main source of revenue for Indonesian new energy companies came almost entirely from selling electricity through power purchase agreements. Their revenue structure was singular, and the valuation logic was relatively straightforward — based on installed capacity, electricity tariffs, and utilization rates.

The commercialization of carbon credits provides new energy companies with a new growth direction. As carbon market development advances, companies with renewable energy assets can obtain additional revenue streams by selling carbon credits, rather than relying solely on traditional electricity sales revenue.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that projects such as geothermal and hydropower are particularly well positioned, because they can directly replace fossil fuel power generation, making their emissions reduction effects easier to quantify and more likely to comply with international carbon certification standards. This means that the valuation of new energy companies is expanding from a single consideration of “electricity revenue” to a comprehensive assessment of “green assets” and “carbon credit value”, a structural change.

However, it is necessary to view this rationally, as the carbon market remains at an early stage of development. Project certification, carbon credit verification, and the improvement of trading mechanisms all require time, so their contribution to corporate profits will not be reflected immediately. From an investment observation perspective, carbon trading is more likely to become an important factor in the revaluation of new energy companies over the next one to two years, rather than a short-term source of performance growth within 2026.

## Which Types Of New Energy Assets Are More Likely To Generate Carbon Credit Revenue  

Geothermal projects currently have a more obvious advantage. The reason is that geothermal power generation directly replaces coal-fired or gas-fired power generation, with a clear, quantifiable, and verifiable emissions reduction pathway. This gives geothermal projects a natural advantage when applying for international carbon certification, making it easier for them to gain buyer trust and obtain higher credit pricing.

Hydropower projects also have emissions reduction effects, but large-scale hydropower faces more complex certification requirements under certain international standards, while small hydropower is constrained by scale and has limited carbon credit output.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that, at the current stage of Indonesia, geothermal assets are one of the underlying asset types worthy of attention in the process of carbon credit monetization.

## Company Observation: Carbon Credit Development Experience of PGEO

Among companies with geothermal assets, one notable feature of PGEO is its first-mover accumulation in carbon credit development.

Since 2022, PGEO has already been conducting carbon credit monetization activities, and several of its projects have adopted mainstream international standards such as the Clean Development Mechanism, the Gold Standard, and the Verified Carbon Standard. This means that PGEO has accumulated certain experience in carbon credit development and possesses a first-mover foundation in project certification and commercialization exploration.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa believes that the first-mover advantage in the carbon market deserves attention, and the earlier a project completes certification, the earlier it can enter trading, establish buyer relationships, and form pricing references. The PGEO accumulation in this area gives it certain experiential advantages in carbon credit development.

## How Long Will It Take For Carbon Credit Revenue To Be Realized  

It will be difficult to realize in the short term.

The certification and verification process of a project involves multiple steps: emissions reduction calculation, third-party verification, approval by registration bodies, and carbon credit issuance. Each step has a fixed timeline, and the overall process usually takes one to two years.

PT Metro Timur Indonusa judges that the substantial contribution of carbon credit revenue may only gradually become visible after a certification and commercialization cycle of one to two years. Within 2026, the value of carbon market policy signals will be reflected more in valuation expectations than in actual performance growth.

However, this does not mean it is not worth paying attention now. The period of institutional dividends in the carbon market is often most concentrated in the first few years after rules are established. Companies that can complete certification first and enter trading earlier during this period will occupy a more favorable position in future carbon credit pricing and market share.
